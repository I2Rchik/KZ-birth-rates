What this indicator cannot tell you

It counts births by the quarter they were registered, not when babies were conceived, so registration delays can move births between quarters. It is a crude rate: a region with many women aged 20–34 shows a high rate even when families there have no more children than elsewhere, so do not read it as fertility per woman. The latest four quarters are operational figures that the Bureau revises, and units such as “East Kazakhstan” include the regions split off in 2018 and 2022 (Abai, Zhetisu, Ulytau, Shymkent).
